WHO WE ARE

More Than Guidance. A Partner in Your Global Education Journey.

Study Upbroad is an international education consultancy dedicated to supporting students who aspire to pursue higher education abroad. We understand that the journey to studying internationally can feel overwhelming — and we are here to make it clearer, manageable, and more confident.

From helping you explore international education opportunities and understand suitable destinations, to identifying the right universities and courses, preparing your applications, discovering scholarship options, and navigating visa and pre-departure requirements — we walk with you at every step.

Our approach is rooted in clarity, honest communication, and genuinely personalized guidance. We do not believe in a one-size-fits-all process. Every student's background, aspirations, and circumstances are different, and our support reflects that.
